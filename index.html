About Me

I am a Biomedical Engineer specialized in Biomechanics with a research trajectory toward Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and intelligent perception. My work focuses on building computational systems that perceive, quantify, and analyze human movement and real-world visual environments.

My technical experience includes human pose estimation, hand tracking, stereo vision, 3D tracking, biosignal processing, real-time computer vision, and robotics-oriented perception. I have worked with YOLO, MediaPipe, OpenCV, and deep-learning pipelines in rehabilitation, human-motion analysis, and human-robot interaction contexts.

My current research interests center on multimodal 3D/4D perception, temporal understanding of human movement, vision-language models, foundation models, embodied AI, and biomedical AI. I am particularly interested in connecting visual perception, biomechanical understanding, and intelligent robotic systems.
