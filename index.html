About Me

I am a Biomedical Engineer specialized in Biomechanics with a research trajectory toward Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and intelligent perception. My work focuses on developing computational systems that can perceive, analyze, and understand human movement and real-world environments.

My technical experience spans human pose estimation, hand tracking, stereo vision, 3D tracking, biosignal processing, medical AI, and robotics. I have developed real-time systems using YOLO, MediaPipe, OpenCV, and modern deep learning architectures, with applications in rehabilitation, human-robot interaction, and biomedical engineering.

I am particularly interested in multimodal 3D/4D perception, temporal understanding of human movement, foundation models, vision-language models, and embodied AI. My long-term goal is to develop robust, generalizable perception systems that connect visual understanding with human-centered and biomedical applications.
